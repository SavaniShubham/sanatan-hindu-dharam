Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Sanatan Dharma (Package: com.sanatanhindudharma.app) mobile application developed for providing devotional content, aartis, bhajans, chalisas, mantras, japa counter, and sacred wallpapers.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We respect your privacy. Our application does not require you to register an account, and we do not collect or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location on any external servers.

All your reading bookmarks, japa mala counts, offline downloaded aartis, and custom alarm preferences are stored locally on your device using secure local storage (MMKV / SQLite).

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the application free for all devotees, our app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve advertisements (Banners, App Open, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads).

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable information, such as:

  • Advertising ID (Google AAID)
  • Device identifiers (model, OS version)
  • App performance and crash diagnostic logs
  • General coarse network location (IP address for regional ad delivery)

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

3. Audio and Media Playback

When you stream devotional audio bhajans or view sacred deity wallpapers, media files are fetched over secure HTTPS connections. No personal listening history is shared with third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The devotional content is suitable for families and devotees of all age groups.

5. Security

We value your trust. We implement standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all network communication with media delivery CDNs and Google services.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We advise you to review this page periodically for any updates.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at: support@sanatandharmaapp.com
